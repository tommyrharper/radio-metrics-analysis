# Interlaced R2D2 DNN Series for Scalable Non-Cartesian MRI with Sensitivity Self-calibration

**Bibcode:** 2025arXiv250309559C
**Authors:** Shijie Chen, Yiwei Chen, Amir Aghabiglou, Motahare Torki, Chao Tang, Ruud B. van Heeswijk, Yves Wiaux
**ADS:** https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025arXiv250309559C/abstract
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09559

## One-line summary
iR2D2 extends the R2D2 "learned Matching Pursuit" DNN-series paradigm (originally from radio-interferometric imaging) to accelerated non-Cartesian multi-coil MRI, adding a bespoke interlaced architecture that alternates between two R2D2 series to jointly self-calibrate coil sensitivity maps and reconstruct the image, governed by an adaptive, error-controlled stopping condition.

## Method
Like R2D2, iR2D2 builds the reconstruction as a series of residual images, each estimated by a DNN module (U-Net or U-WDSR backbone) that takes as input the previous iteration's back-projected data residual (i.e., dirty-image-like residual formed via NUFFT). Because embedding the non-uniform FFT measurement operator inside backpropagation is computationally impractical to train at scale (many coils, higher dimensions), each DNN in the series is trained independently rather than end-to-end/unrolled. Since precomputed sensitivity maps from undersampled k-space are often inaccurate, iR2D2 interlaces two parallel R2D2 series — one refining the image, one refining the sensitivity maps — so the measurement operator used for residual computation is progressively corrected (self-calibration), analogous to closed-loop gain/calibration solving. An adaptive solver stops each series once an error-controlled "sufficient residual energy descent" condition is met, rather than running a fixed number of unrolled iterations. Trained/tested on the FastMRI knee dataset with simulated multi-coil radial (golden-angle) k-space sampling, and validated on real 15-coil 3T bSSFP knee acquisitions.

## Performance / fidelity metrics used
- **PSNR (Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio)**: PSNR(|x⋆|,|x̂|) = 10·log₁₀(NM²/‖|x⋆|−|x̂|‖₂²), computed on reconstructed vs. ground-truth image magnitude (M = max pixel value, N = image size). iR2D2(U-WDSR) achieves **40.21±6.67 dB**, vs. R2D2(U-WDSR) **35.08±4.67 dB** (+5.13 dB gain from interlaced self-calibration), R2D2-Net(U-WDSR) 36.46±5.23 dB, U-WDSR baseline 34.38±4.19 dB, NC-PDNet 34.11±5.01 dB (unrolled non-Cartesian baseline), DDS (diffusion-based) 33.73±6.80 dB, and iR2D2(U-Net) 38.01±6.57 dB. iR2D2 outperforms NC-PDNet by ~6 dB and DDS by ~6.5 dB; the advantage over R2D2 is most pronounced (~6 dB) in the high dynamic-range regime and narrows at low dynamic range where noise dominates.
- **SSIM (Structural Similarity Index Measure)**: standard mean/variance/covariance-based similarity metric (1 = perfect). iR2D2(U-WDSR) 0.96±0.05 vs. R2D2(U-WDSR) 0.93±0.06, R2D2-Net(U-WDSR) 0.94±0.06, U-WDSR 0.92±0.06, NC-PDNet 0.90±0.08, DDS 0.85±0.13.
- **RDR (Residual Data Ratio)**: RDR = ‖r‖₂/‖x_b‖₂, the ℓ2-norm of the current back-projected data residual normalized by the back-projected (dirty) image norm — a data-fidelity/convergence metric used to drive and diagnose the adaptive stopping condition, tracked over series iterations on both simulated and real data.
- **RDR-bar (sensitivity residual)**: an analogous aggregate residual metric for the sensitivity-map series, normalized across coils, quantifying self-calibration convergence/accuracy of the estimated coil sensitivities (no separate ground-truth sensitivity-map metric like NRMSE against ESPIRiT is reported; convergence is assessed via residual descent instead).
- **Reconstruction runtime**: iR2D2(U-WDSR) 5.92±0.38 s vs. R2D2(U-WDSR) 0.68±0.22 s, U-WDSR 0.10±0.03 s, NC-PDNet 0.32±0.01 s, DDS 82.39±6.62 s — iR2D2 is ~14× faster than the diffusion baseline (DDS) despite added self-calibration cost.
- **Model size**: iR2D2(U-WDSR) reported at 315.7M parameters.
- **Training/reconstruction scalability with coil count**: iR2D2 shows a shallower (near-linear) training-time growth with number of coils L than unrolled architectures (e.g. ~4× faster than R2D2-Net at L=64), attributed to avoiding NUFFT operators inside backprop.
- **Convergence/iteration count**: R2D2(U-WDSR) converges at series length i≈3; iR2D2(U-WDSR) at i≈11 (more DNNs needed due to the alternating image/sensitivity structure), with monotonic residual-energy descent enforced by the update condition (UC/UC-bar); an ablation without the update condition shows erratic residual spikes.
- **Real-data validation** (15-coil 3T knee, AF=4 and AF=8): iR2D2 achieves the best (lowest) RDR, roughly an order of magnitude lower than benchmark methods, and qualitatively sharper delineation of anatomical detail (e.g., physeal scar, trabecular bone); residual maps for iR2D2 appear as unstructured noise (indicating successful calibration) whereas benchmarks show structured, anatomy-correlated residuals (indicating model/calibration error).

## Relevance to visibility→gridding→iFFT→deconvolution ML pipeline
iR2D2 is the same R2D2 learned-Matching-Pursuit DNN-series paradigm used for radio-interferometric imaging, here applied to a non-Cartesian (NUFFT-based) Fourier-sampling inverse problem with an added interlaced sensitivity self-calibration loop — directly analogous to jointly solving for antenna/gain calibration errors alongside the image in radio interferometry, and a concrete template for how a residual-series DNN approach could be extended past a fixed measurement operator toward joint calibration-aware imaging.
