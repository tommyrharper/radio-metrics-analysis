# CLEANing Cygnus A Deep and Fast with R2D2

**Bibcode:** 2024ApJ...966L..34D
**Authors:** Arwa Dabbech, Amir Aghabiglou, Chung San Chu, Yves Wiaux (Institute of Sensors, Signals and Systems, Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh)
**ADS:** https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024ApJ...966L..34D/abstract
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03291 (HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2309.03291)

## One-line summary
The paper demonstrates the first application of the R2D2 deep-learning imaging paradigm (and its variants R2D2-Net and R3D3) to real VLA S-band data of the radio galaxy Cygnus A, showing it matches the precision of optimization-based methods (uSARA, AIRI) and exceeds CLEAN's resolution, at a small fraction of the reconstruction cost.

## Method
R2D2 is framed as a learned version of CLEAN in which the minor-cycle deconvolution step is replaced by a sequence of DNN modules, each trained iteration-specifically on the residual dirty image and current image estimate to progressively build up resolution and dynamic range. Three variants are tested: R2D2 (U-Net modules with an exact measurement operator for residual updates), R2D2-Net (an unrolled architecture using PSF-based approximation layers), and R3D3 (a nested structure where R2D2-Net modules act as the minor cycles within an outer major-cycle loop). Networks were trained sequentially per iteration with an L1 loss under a positivity constraint, using a dataset of ~20,000 synthetic ground-truth/dirty-image pairs generated across varied VLA observation configurations. The trained models are then applied, with no fine-tuning, to real Cygnus A S-band VLA visibility data (target dynamic range ~1.7×10^5, super-resolution factor 1.5x relative to nominal pixel size) and benchmarked against CLEAN variants (Högbom, CS-CLEAN, MS-CLEAN) and the optimization-based algorithms uSARA and AIRI.

## Performance / fidelity metrics used
- **Data-fidelity residual standard deviation** (std. dev. of the residual dirty image, linear scale) — the primary quantitative fidelity metric: Hö-CLEAN 359×10⁻⁴; CS-CLEAN 8.6×10⁻⁴; MS-CLEAN 10.4×10⁻⁴; R2D2 11.7×10⁻⁴; R2D2-Net 13.4×10⁻⁴; R3D3 9.7×10⁻⁴; uSARA 7.2×10⁻⁴; AIRI 7.4×10⁻⁴. AIRI, uSARA and CS-CLEAN achieve the lowest (best) residual std. dev.; the R2D2 variants are described as "comparable, slightly above" these top performers.
- **Dynamic range** — target/achieved dynamic range for the Cygnus A reconstruction quoted as ~1.7×10⁵.
- **Super-resolution factor** — imaging pixel size set to 1.5x finer than the nominal (instrumental-resolution) pixel size, used to assess whether methods can resolve super-resolved structure (e.g., continuous AGN-to-jet emission, hotspot morphology).
- **Runtime / computational cost** (seconds, on stated hardware — 1 CPU core for CLEAN/uSARA, 1 GPU for the DNN methods and AIRI): Hö-CLEAN 21.87s (1 iteration); CS-CLEAN 33.69s (7 iterations); MS-CLEAN 39.01s (9 iterations); R2D2 3.31s (15 iterations); R2D2-Net 0.97s (1 iteration); R3D3 1.83s (8 iterations); uSARA 1197s (477 iterations); AIRI 672s (1783 iterations). R2D2 variants deliver roughly an order-of-magnitude speed-up over Högbom CLEAN and 10-20x+ speed-up over uSARA/AIRI.
- **Iteration count** — reported per method (see table above) as a proxy for algorithmic complexity/automation; R2D2-Net needs only 1 (unrolled) iteration, R3D3 needs 8, versus hundreds/thousands for uSARA (477) and AIRI (1783).
- **Qualitative/visual structural comparison** (not a single scalar but used systematically across three regions of Cygnus A — inner core, lobes, hotspots): R2D2 variants show "super-resolved," continuous emission linking the AGN core to both jets where CLEAN variants leave this unresolved; R3D3 and AIRI give the most consistent depiction of faint diffuse lobe emission, with R3D3 appearing sharper on filamentary structure than AIRI; R2D2/R2D2-Net show smoother lobe structure; uSARA introduces wavelet-like artifacts; around hotspots, all R2D2 variants show continuous, physically plausible emission whereas AIRI and uSARA produce artificial zero-valued pixels.
- **Training cost caveat** — the DNN series' upfront training is a "one-off cost" of thousands of CPU-core and GPU hours, but this amortizes since the trained models can then be reused on any future VLA observation without retraining.
- Overall, the paper concludes R2D2 variants (especially R3D3) match AIRI/uSARA's imaging precision and exceed CLEAN's resolution while incurring only a fraction of the reconstruction cost of the optimization-based methods, and without requiring the manual regularization-parameter tuning those methods need.

## Relevance to visibility→gridding→iFFT→deconvolution ML pipeline
This paper is a direct, real-data validation of the R2D2 paradigm (replacing CLEAN's minor-cycle deconvolution with trained DNNs operating on the dirty image/residual), providing concrete baseline numbers (residual std. dev., dynamic range, runtime, iteration count) that a UROP pipeline improving the gridding→iFFT→deconvolution stage with ML could directly compare against or aim to beat.
