# R2D2 Image Reconstruction with Model Uncertainty Quantification in Radio Astronomy

**Bibcode:** 2024arXiv240318052A
**Authors:** Amir Aghabiglou, Chung San Chu, Arwa Dabbech, Yves Wiaux
**ADS:** https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024arXiv240318052A/abstract
**arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18052 (arXiv:2403.18052; HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18052v2; accepted at IEEE EUSIPCO 2024)

## One-line summary
This short paper extends the R2D2 deep-learning imaging paradigm for radio interferometry with model uncertainty quantification via ensemble averaging (5 independently-initialized R2D2 realizations), showing that R2D2 achieves superior reconstruction fidelity to CLEAN, uSARA and AIRI while enabling practical, low-cost pixel-wise epistemic uncertainty maps.

## Method
R2D2 reconstructs an image as a series of residual images, each estimated by a DNN (U-Net based) taking the previous image estimate and its associated back-projected data residual as input: x^(i) = x^(i-1) + N_θ^(i)(r^(i-1), x^(i-1)), with r^(i-1) the dirty residual image formed via the measurement operator Φ. Networks are trained (L1 loss with positivity projection) on 20,000 images (512×512, curated from astronomical and medical images) with per-image dynamic range a ∈ [10³, 5×10⁵] via reversible pixel-wise exponentiation, and a distinct VLA Fourier sampling pattern per training image. To quantify epistemic model uncertainty (uncertainty due to the choice of DNN regularization model, as opposed to aleatoric/data-noise uncertainty), the authors train L=5 independent full R2D2 series from different random DNN initializations; the pixel-wise empirical mean and relative standard deviation across the 5 resulting reconstructions serve respectively as the final image estimate and its uncertainty map. The method is validated on 200 simulated VLA inverse problems and on a real VLA S-band observation of Cygnus A, benchmarked against CLEAN, a standalone U-Net (R2D2's first iteration alone), uSARA, and AIRI.

## Performance / fidelity metrics used
- **SNR (signal-to-noise ratio, dB)** — primary fidelity-to-ground-truth metric, Eq. 6: SNR(x̂, x⋆) = 20·log10(‖x⋆‖₂ / ‖x⋆ − x̂‖₂). Reported progression on simulated test set (Fig. 2): R2D2 converges to ~33 dB versus ~28 dB for the standalone U-Net (R2D2's 1st iteration), 19.5 dB for uSARA, and 15.9 dB for CLEAN. On the real-data-like case study (3C 353, Fig. 3): SNR rises from 35.0 dB (iteration 1, U-Net) to 37.7 dB (final R2D2 iteration, i=12).
- **logSNR (dB)** — SNR computed on a reversible log-transformed image (Eq. 7–8: r_log(x) = x_max·log_a(a/x_max·x + 1), then SNR of the transformed images) to better capture fidelity of faint, high-dynamic-range structure. Simulated test set (Fig. 2, center panel): R2D2 final ~30 dB vs. CLEAN 19.2 dB and uSARA 10.2 dB. Case study (3C 353): rises from 29.2 dB (iteration 1) to 35.3 dB (final iteration).
- **Relative epistemic uncertainty [σ/µ]** — pixel-wise ratio of the standard deviation to the mean image across the L=5 ensemble realizations (Eq. 11), thresholded to zero below 1/(target dynamic range) to suppress noise-floor artifacts. On the simulated test set this drops sharply across the R2D2 series: ~5% (0.05) at iteration 1 (U-Net), ~1.4% at iteration 3, down to ~0.2–0.3% at the final iteration (i=12) — demonstrating that later R2D2 iterations are far more robust/consistent across random initializations than the first-iteration U-Net alone. For the 3C 353 case study: 0.0537 → 0.0139 → 0.0023 across iterations 1, 3, 12. On real Cygnus A VLA data, the final mean-image estimate (averaged over the 5 realizations) achieves a relative uncertainty of only ~0.5%.
- **Computational cost (Table I)** — number of iterations and total wall-clock reconstruction time (mean ± std): U-Net 1 iteration, 0.1±0.1 s; R2D2 12 iterations, 2.0±0.4 s; CLEAN 8±1 iterations, 93.2±26.7 s; AIRI 4995±50 iterations, 3430±1461 s; uSARA 1107±377 iterations, 4015.9±1471.2 s. R2D2 is orders of magnitude faster than CLEAN, uSARA and AIRI, which is what makes computing multiple (L=5) full reconstructions for uncertainty quantification practical at large image dimensions.
- **Qualitative/comparative conclusion**: the paper states R2D2's "image estimation capability is largely superior to that of CLEAN, U-Net, and even uSARA and AIRI," combining higher SNR/logSNR, much lower compute cost, and low epistemic uncertainty relative to the single-iteration U-Net baseline.

## Relevance to visibility→gridding→iFFT→deconvolution ML pipeline
This paper directly targets the deconvolution stage of the pipeline (replacing CLEAN's minor-cycle deconvolution with a DNN series operating on the dirty/residual image from gridding+iFFT) and additionally shows how to obtain calibrated, low-overhead epistemic uncertainty maps for ML-based reconstructions via cheap ensembling — a useful pattern (SNR/logSNR for fidelity, relative-uncertainty maps, iteration count/wall-clock cost) for benchmarking and adding uncertainty quantification to any ML replacement of the deconvolution step in this UROP project's pipeline.
